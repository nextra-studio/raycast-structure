## Qualification

Five generic/template/user-reference materials lack a uniquely matching external specification (CLT-C24, CLT-T14, Plywood-template, Spano-V313-18, Cement-board-12). No blanket correctness claim is made. OSB mean density is an assumption; published characteristic density was compared. Derived G_05 for sawn timber and convenience aliases are not independent standard declarations. Steel presets do not include design strengths. Concrete density 2400 kg/m³ remains an editable assumption. Concrete E uses rounded table values while other equation fields retain unrounded values.

The 26 timber/LVL section dimensions match the supplied source; geometry is independently checked by integration tests. The 11 IPE rows match the cited British Steel edition and its rounding. Other manufacturers/catalogues may differ. Warping/torsion units were checked; engineering approximations for custom geometry are not exact 3D stress solutions.

## Added steel catalogue, 23 October 2026

The two original CSV fingerprints above remain unchanged. A separate `sections-steel.csv` adds 687 published records: IPE 18, HEA/HEB/HEM 24 each, UPN 18, equal L 192, SHS 123, RHS 161 and CHS 103. Eleven existing IPE IDs take precedence; the loaded library contains 713 unique sections.

Every added record is compared with frozen source-table cells in `tests/steel-source-fixtures.json`: 9,162 numeric comparisons, including dimensions, area, inertias, source-rounded moduli and available plastic/torsional properties. CHS area/inertia/torsion are additionally checked by circle equations; SHS/RHS area and both inertias by independent rounded-corner equations. Positive inertia tensors, elastic moduli and mass/area relationships are checked across all sections. Source rounding requires stated tolerances; schematic fillet-free drawings are not the property reference.

The independent import audit caught missing initial equal-angle dimension/thickness rows; the new importer/output were corrected before release. None of the original CSV values were changed. The seven earlier material-field discrepancies and five unverified generic records remain pending.
